# Art direction: stylised rendering (cel shading)

The PRD proposed a "simple, timeless, stylised (cel-shading-like)" style, motivated by longevity and technical compatibility. We keep it rather than faithfully reproducing Mirror's Edge's style: the latter (very high contrast, white/red, detailed city, crafted lighting) needs an AAA art budget, out of reach for a solo dev. REA lets us *describe* the commercial game's techniques, but provides no reusable asset (encrypted Frostbite bundles, and above all a protected work, incompatible with free assets).

## Considered and rejected

- **Reproduce Mirror's Edge's style**: too expensive in art, and impossible to feed with assets from the analysed game.
- **Achievable stylisation + ME's visual language**: an intermediate (flat stylised rendering keeping ME's palette and readability); rejected in favour of cel shading, simpler and already set in the PRD.

## Consequences

The readability language of traversable surfaces (what can be climbed, what can be crossed) must be carried by the style rather than by realistic art. Cel shading must therefore compensate through **colour** and **outline**, not texture detail.
